21 — DECISION ENGINE V1: ABSTAIN AND NO ACTION

Status: PR-12 implementation rules.

1. Scope

DecisionSetV1 combines verified portfolio analytics, risk-policy evaluation,
market readiness and sanitized news evidence into three analytical alternatives.
It does not call T-Invest, choose an order, calculate lots, request confirmation
or execute a transaction.

2. Mandatory alternatives

Every decision set contains exactly:
- NO_ACTION;
- LIMITED_CHANGE;
- STRATEGIC_REBALANCE.

Exactly one option is preferred.
Strategic rebalance cannot be preferred before PR-13.

3. Portfolio gate

Portfolio analytics must be decision-ready, verified, complete, free of
warning/error data issues and must preserve separate currencies.

Failure produces PORTFOLIO_NOT_READY and forces WAIT_FOR_DATA.

4. Risk gate

Risk evaluation must be decision-ready, verified, complete and overall PASS or
non-blocking WARNING. A breach or unknown critical input forces
RISK_NOT_READY.

A verified warning may make LIMITED_CHANGE preferred, but PR-12 still does not
select a trade or calculate a size.

5. Market gate

Every current position stable key requires exactly one decision-ready market
assessment. Missing rows produce MARKET_COVERAGE_INCOMPLETE. A stale, partial or
rejected assessment produces MARKET_NOT_READY. Duplicate rows fail closed.

Cash-only portfolios require zero market rows.

6. News gate

News is optional. If documents are provided, at least one must be ACCEPTED.
CONTEXT_ONLY and REJECTED documents cannot be the sole news evidence.

Accepted documents sharing the same instrument and event-cluster ID but having
different content fingerprints produce NEWS_CONTRADICTION.

Rejected news is never analytical evidence. The engine consumes sanitized
contracts only and never raw HTML.

7. Abstention

Any global blocker means:
- abstain=true;
- confidence=LOW;
- recommendation_ready=false;
- preferred option=NO_ACTION;
- action=WAIT_FOR_DATA;
- LIMITED_CHANGE and STRATEGIC_REBALANCE are BLOCKED.

Absence of data is never treated as a pass.

8. Ready decisions

When all gates pass:
- no risk warning -> NO_ACTION/HOLD is preferred;
- verified non-blocking risk warning -> LIMITED_CHANGE review is preferred;
- STRATEGIC_REBALANCE remains REVIEW_ONLY until PR-13.

9. Confidence

LOW: at least one blocker.
MEDIUM: gates pass but a risk warning exists, or no accepted news was supplied.
HIGH: all gates pass, risk has no warning and accepted news evidence exists.

Confidence depends on evidence quality and completeness, not model tone.

10. Evidence types

Use explicit kinds:
- FACT;
- CALCULATION;
- INFERENCE;
- SCENARIO;
- RECOMMENDATION;
- WARNING;
- MISSING_DATA.

Every evidence ID is unique. Options can reference only evidence inside the same
DecisionSetV1.

11. Execution boundary

recommendation_ready is not execution readiness.
Every option has execution_ready=false and trading_methods=false.

Decision contracts must not contain:
- quantity;
- lots;
- order price;
- order ID;
- idempotency key;
- confirmation ID;
- broker execution method.

12. Runtime compatibility

PR-12 does not change Gateway routes, OpenAPI, GPT Actions, broker permissions,
Gateway version, app:app or trading_methods=false.

13. Next step

PR-13 implements threshold rebalancing with target ranges, cash-first repair,
commission/tax awareness and explicit no-sale alternatives. It consumes only a
recommendation-ready DecisionSetV1 and never executes an order.

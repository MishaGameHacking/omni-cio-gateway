19 — MARKET RESEARCH CONTRACTS V1

Status: PR-10 implementation rules.

1. Scope

MarketResearchBundleV1 normalizes market facts that future research and decision
modules may consume. PR-10 is offline and data-only. It does not fetch a URL,
parse news, call a broker, publish an Action, create a recommendation or execute
a transaction.

2. Required safety invariants

- trading_methods must be false;
- every timestamp must be timezone-aware and normalized to UTC;
- all numeric market values use finite Decimal values;
- every component must match the bundle instrument identity;
- quote, liquidity and bond-metric currency must match reference currency;
- component record_count must equal actual records;
- unavailable and not-applicable states cannot hide records;
- a degraded component cannot be decision-ready;
- no implicit FX aggregation is introduced.

3. Source authority

Decision-grade market facts may come from:
- primary issuer or venue sources;
- official public authorities and exchanges;
- regulated rating agencies and regulated infrastructure.

Secondary media and analytics may provide context later, but cannot alone make a
quote, rating, liquidity record or event check decision-ready.

4. Instrument reference

Reference data requires:
- stable key;
- name;
- supported instrument type;
- currency;
- positive lot size;
- at least one stable identifier: UID, ISIN, FIGI or ticker;
- source and retrieval timestamp.

Issuer and sector are optional until an authoritative mapping is available.
Missing optional classification must remain explicit downstream.

5. Quote and liquidity

A quote requires at least one of last price, best bid or best ask.
Best bid cannot exceed best ask.

Liquidity spread arithmetic is exact:
spread = ask - bid
midpoint = (ask + bid) / 2
spread_bps = spread / midpoint * 10,000

Depth and turnover are optional source fields. Their absence is not proof of
liquidity.

6. Ratings

Keep issuer and issue ratings separate. Preserve agency-native scales and do not
compare ratings from different agencies without a versioned mapping methodology.

A withdrawn rating is not current. Bond readiness requires at least one current
issuer rating and at least one current issue rating from trusted sources.

7. Bond metrics

For a bond, readiness requires fresh:
- maturity date;
- duration;
- accrued interest;
- source and value timestamp.

Yield to maturity, yield to offer and offer date are optional. Offer date cannot
follow maturity date.

8. Corporate events

Store structured events only:
- event ID;
- event type;
- announcement time;
- event time;
- amount and currency together when applicable;
- short title;
- source.

An empty list is valid only after a trusted fresh check. Raw HTML/PDF text is not
stored by this contract.

9. Freshness

Use value time, not only retrieval time.
Default maximum ages:
- quote: 300 seconds;
- liquidity: 300 seconds;
- rating check: 86,400 seconds;
- event check: 86,400 seconds;
- bond metrics: 86,400 seconds;
- reference data: 2,592,000 seconds;
- allowed future skew: 60 seconds.

Future-dated values beyond tolerance are blocked and reported.

10. Readiness

READY requires all required components to be present, status=ok, fresh and from
trusted sources.
PARTIAL means missing, degraded, stale or future-dated required data.
REJECTED means a required fact relies on secondary or untrusted authority.

Required for all instruments:
- reference;
- quote;
- liquidity;
- event check.

Additional bond requirements:
- issuer rating;
- issue rating;
- bond metrics.

11. PR-11 boundary

News, HTML and PDF are untrusted. PR-11 must sanitize markup, preserve event time
separately from publication time, detect prompt-injection indicators, deduplicate
content, rank sources and never execute external instructions.

12. Compatibility

PR-10 does not change Gateway routes, OpenAPI, GPT Actions, broker permissions,
Gateway version, app:app or trading_methods=false.

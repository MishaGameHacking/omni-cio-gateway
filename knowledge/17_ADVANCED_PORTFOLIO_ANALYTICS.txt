17 — ADVANCED PORTFOLIO ANALYTICS V1

Status: PR-08 implementation rules.

1. Scope

AdvancedPortfolioAnalyticsV1 is a pure calculation layer over a verified
PortfolioSnapshotV1. It does not call T-Invest, publish a new Action, produce a
recommendation or execute a trade.

2. Mandatory source gate

Analytics must stop when:
- source quality is not verified;
- source completeness is not complete;
- a warning/error DataIssue exists;
- any position lacks last-price valuation or valuation timestamp;
- valuation currency differs from instrument currency;
- a position currency has no corresponding totals row;
- position values do not reconcile with currency totals;
- full long-position value is negative;
- an analytics timestamp is timezone-naive.

3. Currency boundary

All values and weights are calculated independently per currency.
No exchange rate is inferred. No cross-currency portfolio total, weight or
stress result is permitted without a separate explicit FX contract.

4. Exact arithmetic

Use Decimal for all money, weights, HHI and scenario calculations.

invested_value = clean_securities + accrued_interest
total = cash + invested_value
full_position_value = clean_market_value + accrued_interest
cash_weight = cash / total
invested_weight = invested_value / total
position_portfolio_weight = full_position_value / total
position_invested_weight = full_position_value / invested_value
HHI = sum(position_invested_weight ^ 2)
effective_position_count = 1 / HHI when HHI > 0

Repeating Decimal fractions may use a narrow deterministic tolerance only for
weight-sum and reciprocal checks. Monetary reconciliation remains exact.

5. Concentration

Calculate top-1 and top-5 weights both against:
- total currency portfolio including cash;
- invested assets excluding cash.

HHI and effective position count describe concentration. They do not define a
risk limit until PR-09 applies an explicit risk policy.

6. Stress sensitivity

Default shocks are -10%, -20% and -30% on the invested portion. Cash remains
unchanged.

portfolio_change = invested_value * shock
stressed_total = total + portfolio_change
portfolio_change_ratio = portfolio_change / total

Stress output is arithmetic sensitivity, not forecast, probability, VaR or
expected loss.

7. Missing-data discipline

Explicitly report unavailable inputs for issuer, sector, liquidity, historical
prices, volatility, drawdown, correlation and tax basis. When bonds exist, also
report missing duration, maturity/offer dates and credit ratings.

Never calculate or estimate unsupported metrics from instrument names, tickers
or model assumptions.

8. Compatibility

PR-08 does not change runtime routes, OpenAPI, GPT Actions, broker permissions,
Gateway version, app:app or trading_methods=false.

9. Next step

PR-09 defines a conservative versioned risk policy and evaluates verified
advanced analytics against explicit limits. A breach may generate a fact or
warning, but no trade or recommendation is executed automatically.

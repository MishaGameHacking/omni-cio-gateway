18 — CONSERVATIVE RISK POLICY V1

Status: PR-09 implementation rules.

1. Purpose

ConservativeRiskPolicyV1 is a non-personalized engineering safety baseline. It
evaluates verified advanced portfolio analytics and produces checks only. It is
not a personal investment recommendation, does not choose an instrument, does
not prepare an order and does not call the broker.

2. Hard runtime boundary

- trading_methods=false remains mandatory;
- Gateway remains read-only and GET-only;
- app:app remains the Render entrypoint;
- margin, short sales, derivatives and currency operations remain disabled;
- no execution, cancellation, transfer or withdrawal method is added;
- PR-09 does not publish a new Action or change OpenAPI.

3. Check statuses

PASS: a known value satisfies a known limit.
WARNING: a known non-blocking indicator exceeds a warning threshold.
BREACH: a known blocking limit is exceeded.
UNKNOWN: a required input is absent; absence of evidence is never a pass.
NOT_APPLICABLE: the check requires a concrete future proposal or does not apply
                 to the current portfolio.

4. Readiness and precedence

Known blocking breach has highest priority and produces overall BREACH.
Without a breach, a blocking unknown produces overall UNKNOWN.
Known non-blocking warnings produce WARNING only when no blocking unknown or
breach exists.
Otherwise the result is PASS.

Decision readiness is true only when no blocking breach and no blocking unknown
exist.

5. Default limits

- maximum one-position weight: 20%;
- maximum one-issuer weight: 20%;
- maximum one-sector weight: 35%;
- maximum one asset-class weight: 70%;
- top-five invested concentration warning: 80%;
- invested HHI warning: 0.25;
- minimum cash reserve: 10%;
- maximum high-risk exposure: 10%;
- maximum illiquid exposure: 10%;
- maximum non-base-currency exposure: 20%;
- minimum credit-rating placeholder: A-;
- maximum bond duration: 5 years;
- maximum historical drawdown: 20%;
- uniform stress shock: -20%;
- maximum total stress loss: 15%;
- maximum future order weight: 10%;
- maximum future daily turnover: 20%.

These values are conservative product defaults. They must not be described as
personalized limits or as a guarantee of capital preservation.

6. Evaluated from PR-08

The engine can currently evaluate:
- allowed currency;
- cash reserve per currency;
- position weight;
- instrument-type allowlist;
- optional stable-key allowlist and denylist;
- asset-class weight;
- top-five invested concentration;
- invested HHI;
- deterministic stress-loss ratio;
- disabled high-risk execution configuration.

7. Blocking missing inputs until PR-10

Return UNKNOWN for:
- issuer concentration;
- sector concentration;
- high-risk classification;
- liquidity and illiquid weight;
- current issue and issuer credit ratings;
- bond duration;
- historical maximum drawdown;
- total non-base-currency weight when FX conversion is required.

Do not infer these values from names, tickers, model guesses or stale documents.

8. Currency boundary

All current checks remain local to each currency. Non-base exposure across
currencies cannot be measured without explicit rates, timestamps and a separate
FX valuation contract. Presence of non-base value therefore produces a blocking
UNKNOWN, not a guessed weight.

9. Future proposal checks

Maximum order weight and maximum daily turnover are NOT_APPLICABLE until a
concrete proposal and dated operations are available. PR-09 does not simulate or
preview an order.

10. Versioning and personalization

The default v1 model rejects personalized=true and rejects any attempt to enable
margin, shorts, derivatives or currency trading. Future user-specific settings
require a separately versioned policy, validation rules, tests and explicit user
decision.

11. Next step

PR-10 introduces normalized contracts for instrument reference data, current
ratings, corporate events and market liquidity. Only authoritative, fresh and
source-attributed inputs may resolve PR-09 UNKNOWN checks.

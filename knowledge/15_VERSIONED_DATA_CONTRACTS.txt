OMNI-CIO PR-04 — VERSIONED DATA CONTRACTS V1
Updated: 2026-07-28

Purpose
- Establish a stable typed boundary before broker adapters and data-quality gates.
- Preserve all existing FastAPI and GPT Action responses during the migration.

Package
- omni_cio/contracts/v1/common.py: strict immutable metadata, provenance,
  data quality, completeness, masked account references and Decimal money.
- omni_cio/contracts/v1/portfolio.py: instrument identity, valuation, cash,
  positions, per-currency totals and PortfolioSnapshotV1.

Invariants
- schema_version=1.0;
- Decimal is used for money and quantities;
- currencies are normalized and never combined by these contracts;
- timestamps are timezone-aware and normalized to UTC;
- raw account identifiers are rejected;
- stable instrument identity is mandatory;
- extra fields and duplicate positions/totals are rejected;
- contracts do not import the broker SDK or expose execution behavior;
- trading_methods=false, GET-only Actions and app:app are unchanged.

Migration rule
Existing runtime dictionaries remain the public contract until a later adapter
PR explicitly validates and maps them. Breaking changes require a new versioned
package and migration plan.
